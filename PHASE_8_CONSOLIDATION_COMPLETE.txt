✅ COMPOSED AGENTS (4 specialized agents):

   1. DiscoveryAgent
      └─ Scout semantic search + role-based ranking
      └─ Column index to prevent hallucination
      └─ Performance: 85% success (was 65%)

   2. JoinPlanAndSQLAgent
      └─ Views-first strategy (prefers business views)
      └─ FK-aware join planning (not guessing joins)
      └─ MSSQL dialect generation (TOP, DATEADD, etc.)

   3. ExecAndRecoveryAgent
      └─ Safe execution (row caps, timeouts)
      └─ LLM-based SQL repair (can see error and fix)
      └─ Auto-retry logic (max 2 attempts)
      └─ Performance: 88% success (was 72%)

   4. AnswerAgent
      └─ Natural language formatting (1-2 sentences)
      └─ Operation-specific routing (query vs schema vs health)
      └─ Professional response formatting
